## Search-result research and content decisions

Queries examined: asphalt calculator square feet to tons; 1 ton asphalt m2 calculator; asphalt density tons per cubic yard; tack coat spray rate calculator; tons of asphalt per cubic yard calculator; asphalt millings coverage per ton. The web search results are a directional competitor sample, not a controlled Google location/device rank export.

Observed calculator providers commonly offer dimensions, depth, density, a formula and a worked example. They sometimes mix loose and compacted density assumptions. BitumenCalc's useful distinction is to make the material condition, ton unit, and allowance explicit and keep the reference tables consistent with the live tool.

Sample pages examined:
- https://www.othercalculators.com/calculators/asphalt/asphalt-square-feet-to-tons-calculator
- https://steeltoetools.com/blog/asphalt-density-tons-per-cubic-yard
- https://housebuildcostcalculator.com/asphalt-milling-calculator/
- https://calcady.com/trade/construction/asphalt-tack-coat-rate

No competitor copy or unverified prices were reused. Technical support comes from NIST, FHWA, the Asphalt Institute and relevant road authorities; numerical worked examples are independently calculated.

## SEO, AEO and GEO implementation

- Four excluded guides rewritten with distinct questions, concise opening answers, unit-labelled tables, original worked examples, contextual calculator links, and visible FAQs.
- FAQ structured data mirrors the actual page answers. It does not promise FAQ rich results or AI citations.
- Main calculators retain inputs and core arithmetic; explanatory content clarifies assumptions.
- The homepage owns general bitumen mixture/binder estimation; the directory helps choose a tool rather than repeating that primary target.
- Titles, canonicals, sitemap lastmod, logo markup and broken legacy redirects are addressed.
- No machine-only AI files, artificial mentions, doorway routes or special AI schema were added.

Google guidance:
https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features
https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide

Indexing and snippet eligibility are prerequisites for supporting links in Google's AI search features. Google provides no special schema requirement for this. Search Console includes AI-feature traffic in overall Web performance and does not provide a separate reliable “AI Overview rank” in these tools.
